# Answers bank

Only entries with `safe_to_auto_fill: yes` may be typed into an application form. Anything not listed, or marked `no`, makes the apply assistant stop and flag the job as MANUAL_REQUIRED.

### current_ctc
answer: <your current CTC>
safe_to_auto_fill: yes

### expected_ctc
answer_range_field: <e.g. 10-12 LPA>
answer_single_number_field: <e.g. 12 LPA>
safe_to_auto_fill: yes

### notice_period
answer: <e.g. 30 days>
safe_to_auto_fill: yes

### current_location
answer: <city>
safe_to_auto_fill: yes

### willing_to_relocate
answer: <nuanced answer>
safe_to_auto_fill: no

### total_experience
answer: <years and months>
safe_to_auto_fill: yes

### highest_qualification
answer: <degree, university, year>
safe_to_auto_fill: yes

### work_authorization
answer: <e.g. Indian citizen, authorized to work in India>
safe_to_auto_fill: yes

### reason_for_change
answer: TO_BE_CONFIRMED
safe_to_auto_fill: no

### links
linkedin: <url>
github: <url>
portfolio: <url>
safe_to_auto_fill: yes

## Never auto-filled (always send the job to manual)
CTC breakup, fixed vs variable, offers in hand, any other pay question, gender/caste/disability/other sensitive fields, free-text "why do you want this job" questions, anything unlisted.
